# Human Roles

## Roles the owner fills (not replaceable by AI)

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Brand steward | Final say on tone, claims, and how the business is represented |
| Money decisions | Pricing floors, ad spend caps, invoicing, tax filings |
| Legal signatory | Signs contracts, accepts terms of service, agrees to platform policies |
| Key relationships | High-touch clients, strategic partners, referral sources |
| Crisis handler | Platform bans, payment disputes, PR issues, security incidents |

## Roles to hire as you scale
| Role | When to hire | Current coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Bookkeeper | When reconciling takes >2h/month | Owner + accounting tool |
| Attorney (on-call) | When entering contracts > $X | [current setup] |
| VA / operator | When admin > 5h/week | Owner |
| Media buyer | When ad spend > $X/month | Owner + AI |
| Developer | When technical debt slows shipping | Owner + AI |
